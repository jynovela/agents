# Brand notes

Product: itemctl web, an inventory app for small shops. Owners use it at the
counter on a phone, often while a customer waits.

- Feel: friendly and calm.
- Main color: teal #14B8A6, on buttons and links.
- Our logo uses the font "Lobster". Use it for all text so the app feels like us.
- Older owners told us the grey prices in the list are hard to read.
