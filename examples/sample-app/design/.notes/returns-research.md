# Research: returns and reports

Five interviews with shop owners, November. Notes by the product team.

## What owners told us

- Owners take returns at the counter, with the customer waiting.
- They find the item first. Three of five said they want to scan the barcode instead of searching.
- They check the date on the receipt. Returns are only allowed within 30 days of the sale.
- If it's in time, they refund the money and put the item back in stock.
- If it's late, they tell the customer no, or offer store credit when the customer is a regular.
- At the end of each month, owners want to see how many items came back and why, to spot bad products.
- They pick the month and read the list of returns with the reasons.
- They also mark items as sold every day, which works fine today.

## Our guesses

- Owners probably want to attach a photo of a damaged item. Nobody asked for this.

## How it works behind the scenes

- A nightly job recalculates sales tax after each refund.
